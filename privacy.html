Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Coined is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information — and there is nothing to opt out of, because nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. Coined makes no network requests, includes no analytics, and contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Your saved words, streaks, preferences, and Curator selections are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud backup, if you have one enabled).

Purchases

The optional one-time "Curator Unlock" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Coined never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Coined does not collect data from anyone, including children.
