Coined – Support

Coined gives you three word origins every day, each verified against real etymological sources. Here are answers to the most common questions.

When do new words arrive?

Three new etymologies unlock each day at midnight local time. Your streak resets if you miss a day, but your saved words are never lost.

What is the Curator Unlock?

The optional one-time Curator Unlock lets you choose the category of words you see each day — selecting from themes like Science, Literature, or Everyday Speech. Without it, Coined curates a balanced daily mix for you automatically.

Does Coined require an internet connection?

No. All word content is bundled with the app. You can explore your saved words and review past etymologies entirely offline.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Coined, tap the settings icon, and choose "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used when you bought Curator Unlock.

My streak reset — can it be recovered?

Streaks are stored on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app without an iCloud backup, the streak cannot be recovered. We're sorry — please reach out and we'll note your feedback for a future iCloud sync feature.
